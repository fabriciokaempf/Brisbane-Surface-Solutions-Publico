2 · Google Ads

One Search campaign, built for commercial work

Giving access

  1. Sign in at ads.google.com with the business account.
  2. Admin › Access and security › the blue + button.
  3. Enter the agency's email and choose Admin or Standard. If they use a manager (MCC) account, accept their link request in Admin › Access and security › Managers instead.

What is set up, and why

  • One non-brand Search campaign, Phrase and Exact match only. Broad match was left out on purpose until the account has more conversion volume.
  • Daily budget A$33. It was raised for a few weeks in August and September and brought back on 24 September, because the extra spend did not return extra enquiries. The account re-entered learning on that date.
  • Locations: Brisbane and surrounding councils, plus Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast (added in August). Gold Coast has converted; Sunshine Coast has shown little demand so far.
  • Conversions, measured through Tag Manager: Lead - Quote Form (Site) is the primary conversion. Phone Click (Site) is secondary since 28 July, so the bidding optimises for quote requests. Anyone comparing weeks across 28 July should use the "All conversions" column.
  • Do not import GA4 conversions into Google Ads. The same actions are already measured through Tag Manager, and importing them would double count.
  • Negative keywords at campaign level: job seekers, DIY, training, equipment for sale or hire, and services the business does not offer (handyman, polished or decorative floors, pools, gutters, stumps, among others). "driveway" is blocked by decision: residential demand pulls budget away from the commercial work that converts.
  • Auto-apply recommendations: off. Keep it off; it undoes deliberate settings.
  • The biggest lever found in September: the campaign loses more auctions to ad rank than to budget. Quality and relevance will move results more than extra spend.
Worth checking: one of the ad callouts reads "Fully Insured". Confirm the current insurance cover with the owner before keeping it live.

3 · Google Business Profile

Live, verified and showing in public search

Giving access

  1. Search "Brisbane Surface Solutions" on Google while signed in with the business account, or open business.google.com.
  2. Three dots › Business Profile settings › People and access › Add.
  3. Enter the agency's email and choose Manager. Keep the business as the only Owner.

What is set up

  • Primary category Concrete contractor, three secondary categories, 13 services, 7 service areas across South East Queensland, hours 7:00 to 16:00, logo, cover and 19 photos.
  • Linked to Google Ads in September (location asset active at account level).
  • The real gap is reviews. The profile has none yet, while nearby competitors have 26 to 33. Review link to send to past clients: g.page/r/CclnQXDUTSAsEBM/review

4 · Website

WordPress, Elementor and Rank Math

Giving access

  1. Sign in to the WordPress dashboard with the main Administrator login (the one sent on 27 September).
  2. Users › Add New User. Use the agency's email and the role they need (Editor for content, Administrator for technical work).

What was fixed in September

Full before and after: Site report, September 2026. In short: the Google title now leads with the business name, the footer email link works, the "residential" wording was removed, and the service areas section (including Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast), which existed but was hidden by a code error, now shows.

Before touching anything

  • There are pending plugin and core updates. Take a full backup first. They were deliberately left alone without one.
  • Two users other than the main Administrator exist: one unknown account was reduced to Subscriber, and the outgoing manager's Administrator account will be removed once the handover is confirmed.
  • A Privacy Policy draft exists but was never published. Publishing an approved version unlocks Enhanced Conversions in Google Ads (a ready plan exists if the team wants it).
  • The site's Tag Manager snippet (GTM-KT5P58T3) is installed on the site; do not remove it while the conversions above are in use.

5 · Google Tag Manager

The one asset that needs a transfer

The container GTM-KT5P58T3 holds the conversion tags for the quote form and phone clicks. It sits under the outgoing manager's Google account. To hand it over cleanly:

  1. The outgoing manager adds the business Google account as Administrator of the Tag Manager account, with Publish permission on the container.
  2. The owner accepts the invitation and then adds the agency with the permissions they need.
  3. Once the owner confirms access, the outgoing manager removes their own user.

Nothing on the site changes during this process, and measurement keeps running throughout.

6 · Instagram + Facebook

Content published through Meta Business Suite

Giving access

  1. Open business.facebook.com with the account that manages the Page.
  2. Settings › Partners (if the agency has its own business portfolio) or People (to add a person), then assign the Facebook Page and the Instagram account.

What to know

  • The Instagram account was linked to the Facebook Page through a former contractor's personal Facebook profile. Scheduling works today, but the agency may run into it when changing settings. Do not unlink anything while posts are scheduled.
  • Posts for the first half of October may already be scheduled in the Planner. Check there before publishing, so nothing doubles up.
  • Every post since July follows one visual system (navy and orange, hazard stripe, a red palette reserved for silica and compliance) and an editorial line aimed at builders, project managers and fit-out teams: Editorial line.
  • A safety rule from the owner, for every photo: no cable, lead or hose lying loose on the floor. A hose attached to a machine in use is fine.
  • Public name in content is "Andrew", at the owner's request.
